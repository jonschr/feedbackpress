# FeedbackPress interface

Inline editing: double-click editable title/description/reply text in an open ticket to replace just that field with the existing input/textarea and no action buttons, preserving the other field and reply draft. Scope permission to the same immutable ownership as menu Edit, regardless of displayed attribution. Owner-private notes reuse their body editor. Leave links and interactive descendants native. Editable text does not trigger single-click marker reveal, so an offscreen marker cannot move the card between clicks. Escape cancels the inline field, including its mention popup, without dismissing the ticket/sidebar even after focus drifts. Clicking elsewhere inside that ticket or Cmd/Ctrl+Enter saves; generic blur or clicking outside the ticket does not. The first non-link outside-field click commits and is consumed to keep the ticket/selection stable; links still activate. Retain invalid/stale drafts and focus, skip unchanged writes, and lock private fields briefly while saving to prevent duplicate submissions or late typing. Keep full Save/Cancel Edit in every applicable issue/reply/private-note menu; shared-only replies use the same Edit-only menu rather than a standalone button. Reuse revision-checked full payloads and the current failure/conflict Restore draft flow, which reopens the full editor. Text-change comparisons remain specialized and context-body edits preserve proposed wording.

Page notes: a large drop area appears halfway down the left edge of the available page viewport only during ordinary ticket placement (C/Leave feedback) or an active pin drag. Hovering the creation area switches the circular placement cursor to the matching square page-note cursor; leaving restores the circle. Styling: 22px strong text and its thick dotted border switch together between translucent black on light backgrounds and white on medium/dark backgrounds. Keep the surface transparent, without text strokes or dual-tone edging. Sample the page beneath the label, using background colors, gradient estimates or readable image pixels; choose light ink early (brightness below 180/255), independently of the widget appearance preference. Recompute on layout/scroll/background changes only while visible. Keep a small bounded image cache and fall back safely when pixel access is blocked. Click the area during placement to create a page note, or drop a regular pin there to convert it. Hide it once a composer opens, on cancellation, or when a drag ends. Keep keyboard activation without introducing visible controls. Existing numbered square page-note markers stay in a chronological stack vertically centered along the left margin while reviewing/scrolling; place the drop area 12px to the right of the measured stack and constrain its width to the available page viewport; opening the sidebar or simply having page notes does not show the drop area. Respect current path/query, filters, selected-ticket visibility and either sidebar side. Reuse empty-selector targets and revision-checked target updates; preserve number, content, author/ownership, page, privacy and conversation. Drag a page-note marker onto an element to position it again. Text-change suggestions retain their element targets. Failed saves and Escape/pointer cancellation restore the original presentation.
